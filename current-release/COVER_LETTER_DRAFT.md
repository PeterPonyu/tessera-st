Dear Editor,

Please consider "Neighborhood averaging accounts for spatial gains of a graph encoder in tumor spatial omics" as an Article for Scientific Reports.

The study tests attribution of spatial clustering gains in one released graph encoder using matched neighborhood means and component ablations. Eight independently annotated breast patients and a separate three-patient colorectal pilot add evidence beyond the primary panel. Weak gate discrimination in the colorectal pilot does not establish a gain over raw features or neighborhood averaging.
