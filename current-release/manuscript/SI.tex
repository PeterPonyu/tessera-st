\section*{S3. Row-order sensitivity from assigning ordinal ranks to ties}
The unchanged historical eleven-object panel is a numerical counterexample
only. For each of 200 row permutations, the joint partial association uses
ranked covariates for class count, feature dimension and modality, an
intercept, separate OLS rank residuals and their Pearson correlation.
Assigning ordinal ranks to equal values makes this output row-order
dependent; average midranks preserve ties (Table~\ref{tab:ranks}). The range
is across computational row orders, not an uncertainty interval. Correct
ranking does not repair the historical graph or contaminated control and
does not establish a tissue-level mechanism.

\section*{S4. Coupled changes in the label-regeneration intervention}
The endpoint audit recomputes generator quantities without fitting STAGATE.
It reuses two substrates, five seed identities and intervention levels zero
and one. Coordinates and the conditional expression-generation rule are
fixed, while selected labels are redrawn and expression regenerated from
the current labels. The operation changes class frequencies, contiguity
and expression spatial autocorrelation together. Tables~\ref{tab:endpoints}
and \ref{tab:seed-endpoints} report the substrate means and all 20 seed-level
endpoint records, respectively. They are not additional independent
tissues or independent-contiguity estimates.

Expression Moran's $I$ is averaged across features on the fixed directed
six-neighbour graph. Residual SD is the population-form standard deviation
of expression minus its generated label-conditioned mean. ``Small class''
is the final generator class, not a retrospectively selected minority.
Random same-label probability is $\sum_c n_c(n_c-1)/[n(n-1)]$, where $n_c$
is the generated count for class $c$ and $n$ is the substrate's cell count.
All four readouts are dimensionless except residual SD, which retains the
synthetic expression scale. Level is the label-redraw fraction, not measured
expression--position alignment.
