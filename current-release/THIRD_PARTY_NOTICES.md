# Third-party materials and redistribution boundaries

The MIT licence covers Zeyu Fu's software, not upstream methods or assays.
Author-written text, tables and author-generated aggregates are CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source photographs and reference
labels embedded in scientific figures remain attributed to their source
publications. The blanket author licence does not override third-party rights.

Raw H5AD/molecular measurement matrices, downloaded STAGATE/SEDR source,
weights and stand-alone source photographs/segmentations are not bundled.
Prediction/selection/coordinate maps are derived audit artifacts, linked to
the exact source identities and subject to the upstream source terms.
The committed scientific vector figures are retained for reading the article;
reuse of source-image portions must follow the original source's conditions.
Historical code in this public development repository remains historical;
`current-release/` is the version-specific scientific release.

Upstream implementations: STAGATE (https://github.com/QIFEIDKN/STAGATE),
SEDR (https://github.com/JinmiaoChenLab/SEDR), Scanpy, Squidpy, scikit-learn,
NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch and PyTorch Geometric. Obtain upstream implementations
separately under their own licences; no right to relicense them is claimed.
Arial is an external system font, not distributed here.
