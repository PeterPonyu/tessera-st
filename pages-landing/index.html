What the package does

The release audits coordinate-frame identities, genuinely expression-only controls and the interpretation of specified synthetic interventions. Tessera is one audited procedure, not a validated superior architecture or a deployment-ready selector.

  • Adaptive edge gating (off: plain mean aggregation)
  • Multi-scale fusion (off: last layer only)
  • Boundary-contrastive loss (off: reconstruction only)
  • Legacy confidence output withheld; no validated confidence claim
